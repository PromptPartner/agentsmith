## Recommendation

Use **Option 1: evidence first** as the shared message stack.

> **Your AI agent is capable. Prove the result.**  
> **Proof before done.**

It begins by agreeing with the audience: the model is capable. The second sentence then reveals the
missing responsibility without insulting the user or the model. It carries all three pillars inside
the extended copy and works for both first-time and experienced builders.

Use Options 2–5 as situational campaigns, not competing master positions:

- Option 2 for scope problems and recovery.
- Option 3 for technical proof and developer education.
- Option 4 for long-running projects and context loss.
- Option 5 for guided first use and non-developer outreach.

## Copy guardrails

- “Proof” always names the evidence and its coverage limit.
- “Control” never promises perfect agent obedience in attended sessions.
- “Continue” means durable project artifacts, not infinite model memory.
- “Autonomous” always includes scope, attempts, checks, stop condition, and authority boundary.
- “For first-time builders” remains a design target until the guided sandbox is observed with users.
- Keep quantified outcome claims out until the case evidence supports them.
- The five options are message variants, not five product positions.
